# StockPro V5 interface system

Pinned by user specification: institutional research workbench in Operate mode. Replaces the old terminal ornament while retaining native Streamlit controls and working analysis.

Scene: sustained financial research on a laptop; dark navy/slate surfaces reduce visual distraction. Background #0B1220; secondary #121D30; border #2B3A50; body #E6EDF5; secondary #AAB8CA; selection #74A8EC; warning #E7BB65. Positive #79C4A5 and negative #EE9A9A carry signed measured changes, never trade instructions. System sans, 16px body, 24–30px page headings; tabular numerals. Thin borders, 6px corners, no gradients/shadows/motion. Native responsive columns and accessible labels.

First viewport: ticker/current completed close/cutoff, selected horizon, central implied return and price, 50/80% bands and probability calibration state. Critical research/PIT/OOD/data warnings precede outputs. Evidence, contributions, scenarios, analogue distribution and full ledger use labeled sections/tabs. Forecast endpoints are independent; do not invent a compounded daily path.

Navigation: Dashboard; Research; Forecast; Validate; Risk & Portfolio; Markets. Existing legacy tools remain discoverable under these groups. Validate separates forecast-model evidence from strategy research and shows quarantined candidates.
